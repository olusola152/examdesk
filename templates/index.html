Set, write and mark exams online.

Lecturers set objective and theory questions. Students write under a timed, auto-saving exam session. Every script is submitted to the lecturer in charge for marking and result release.

I'm a Lecturer I'm a Student

How it works

  1. Lecturer creates an exam and adds Section A (objective) and Section B (theory) questions.
  2. Lecturer opens the exam and shares a 6-character access code.
  3. Students enter the code, start the timer, and answer. Work auto-saves.
  4. Objectives are marked instantly; the lecturer marks theory scripts.
  5. Lecturer releases results and exports the score sheet.
